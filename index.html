About Us

Feather Touch Foundation School & Day Care Centre

Feather Touch Foundation School and Day Care Centre is a nurturing early childhood education centre dedicated to providing quality foundational education in a safe, caring and stimulating environment.

Our school caters to young learners during their most important developmental years through a combination of play-based learning, structured classroom experiences, creative activities and social interaction.

We believe that education at this stage should not be about pressure or rote learning. It should be about curiosity, discovery, confidence and joy.

Our Vision

To create a happy, safe and stimulating learning environment where every child gets the opportunity to discover their potential, develop confidence and build strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Discover Our Approach

Our Mission

  • To provide quality early childhood education.
  • To nurture every child's individual strengths and interests.
  • To make learning joyful, experiential and meaningful.
  • To develop communication, creativity and problem-solving skills.
  • To promote positive social behaviour and emotional development.
  • To create awareness about kindness, respect, responsibility and safety.
  • To build a strong partnership between school and parents.
