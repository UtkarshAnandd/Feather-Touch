# Feather Touch Foundation School & Day Care Centre

> **Nurturing Little Minds, Building Strong Foundations.**

A responsive, child-friendly website for **Feather Touch Foundation School & Day Care Centre**, designed to present the school's programmes, learning approach, activities, child-safety practices, admissions information, and parent engagement in a clear and welcoming way.

> **Status:** 🚧 Actively under development

---

## 🌱 About the Project

Feather Touch Foundation School & Day Care Centre focuses on creating a safe, caring, and stimulating environment where young children can learn, explore, and grow.

This website is being developed to provide the school with a modern digital presence while keeping the experience:

- Warm and welcoming
- Child-friendly
- Parent-focused
- Easy to navigate
- Responsive across devices
- Accessible and readable
- Accurate and trustworthy

The current version establishes the core website structure and design system. The project will continue through multiple UI/UX, responsive-design, content, and accessibility iterations before final delivery.
