   Never inject sample phone numbers (e.g. +91 99999 99999) or fake addresses. Keep the draft conditional logic (`{% if site.contact.phoneDisplay %}`) so that fallback messaging displays cleanly until authentic data is entered.

The redesign should create high-aesthetic, warm placeholder treatments (illustrations, iconography, or subtle abstract patterns) until official photos with verified consent are provided.

   The list of 7 teaching methods, 6 developmental dimensions, 8 safety topics, and 19 celebrations represent real curriculum activities practiced by the school. These must remain intact and prominently featured.
